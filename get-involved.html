Get Involved

This guide gets stronger when working students contribute what is actually working in their week.

Three ways to plug in

Share what works for you

If you have a system that helps you manage the semester, send it. Real systems from real working students improve this site over time.

Suggest a system

Is there a problem the four systems do not cover yet? Suggestions for what to add are welcome.

Pass the link

If a classmate is carrying a heavy load this semester, send them the link. Sometimes a small starting point helps.
