Working Through College at Cal State LA

A guide for working students. Four systems for managing the semester when your week is already full.

Read the Four Systems Why this exists

Who this is for

This site is for Cal State LA students who work while in school. The ones working 30 or 40 hours a week. The ones with family responsibilities. The ones with religious or community commitments outside of class. Students who already know that generic study advice does not always fit their week.

According to U.S. News (2024), 86 percent of first-year students at Cal State LA receive need-based financial aid. Financial pressure is common here, not rare. Most college advice online assumes a student lives on campus and has school as their only responsibility. That is not the reality for most students at Cal State LA.

The Four Systems

A starting point. Pick the one that maps to the problem hurting your week most.

1. Build a Realistic Schedule

Map fixed blocks first. Work, classes, commute, sleep, and other commitments. What is left is what you actually have to work with.

Learn more
2. Manage Your Energy

An hour after a closing shift is not the same as an hour Saturday morning. Match harder work to your better focus windows.

Learn more
3. Communicate Early

Reach out to managers and professors before a conflict happens, not after. Five days early often goes further than the day-of.

Learn more
4. Adjust Course Load When Needed

Reducing a class load is not failure. Sometimes the right move is adjusting before grades and health start to slip.
